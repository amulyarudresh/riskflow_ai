# RiskFlow AI - Data Privacy SLA

**Version:** 2.1

## 1. Compliance
RiskFlow AI maintains full compliance with GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act) for all stored customer data.

## 2. Data Retention and Deletion
- **System Logs:** Retained for 90 days.
- **Database Backups:** Retained for 1 year.
- **Right to be Forgotten:** Data deletion requests will be fully processed across all primary databases and live backups within 30 days of an explicit written request.

## 3. Incident Response
In the event of a suspected or confirmed data breach, RiskFlow AI commits to the following notification SLAs:
- **Critical (Production data exposed):** Notification to affected clients within 4 hours.
- **High (Internal systems compromised without data exposure):** Notification within 24 hours.
